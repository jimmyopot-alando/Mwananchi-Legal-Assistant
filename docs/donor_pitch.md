# 💡 Donor Pitch - Mwananchi Legal Assistant

## 1. Vision
Mwananchi Legal Assistant is an **AI-powered legal platform** built on the MERN stack with Python microservices. Our mission is to **democratize access to justice** by providing affordable, reliable, and easy-to-use legal guidance for citizens across Kenya and beyond.

---

## 2. The Problem
- Millions of citizens lack affordable access to legal services.
- Court processes are complex, slow, and intimidating for ordinary people.
- Legal fees are often unclear, leading to financial strain.
- Rural and marginalized communities face barriers in accessing courts and legal information.

---

## 3. Our Solution
- **AI Legal Guidance**: Citizens can ask questions and receive clear, plain-language answers.  
- **Document Generator**: Automated drafting of affidavits, contracts, and petitions.  
- **Court Finder (GPS)**: Locates nearby courts and provides directions.  
- **Fee Calculator**: Transparent breakdown of filing, service, and hearing fees.  
- **Payment Integration (M-Pesa)**: Secure, seamless payment for legal services.  
- **Metrics Dashboard**: Tracks usage, performance, and impact for continuous improvement.

---

## 4. Impact
- **Accessibility**: Legal help available 24/7 via web and mobile.  
- **Affordability**: Reduces costs by automating routine legal tasks.  
- **Transparency**: Clear fee structures and court information.  
- **Empowerment**: Citizens gain confidence navigating the justice system.  
- **Scalability**: Designed to expand regionally and internationally.  

---

## 5. Sustainability
- Revenue streams from premium features (advanced document drafting, lawyer consultations).  
- Partnerships with law firms, NGOs, and government institutions.  
- Open-source foundation ensures community contributions and long-term growth.  

---

## 6. Call to Action
We invite donors to join us in **transforming access to justice**. Your support will:
- Fund infrastructure scaling and microservice optimization.  
- Expand outreach to rural communities.  
- Enhance AI models for local languages and legal contexts.  
- Ensure sustainability and measurable impact.  

---

**Together, we can make justice accessible for every mwananchi (citizen).**
